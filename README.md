# Integral Representations and Residues in Multidimensional Complex Analysis

L. A. Aĭzenberg and A. P. Yuzhakov, *Integral Representations and Residues
in Multidimensional Complex Analysis*, Translations of Mathematical
Monographs 58. American Mathematical Society, Providence, Rhode Island,
1983. ISBN 0-8218-4511-X. Translated from the Russian edition (Nauka,
Novosibirsk, 1979) by H. H. McFaden; translation edited by Lev J. Leifman.

The book treats integral representations of holomorphic functions of
several complex variables and the theory of multidimensional residues: the
Martinelli–Bochner, Leray–Koppelman, Cauchy–Fantappiè, Andreotti–Norguet,
Bergman–Weil and Martinelli–Bochner–Koppelman formulas, the multidimensional
logarithmic residue and the multiplicity of a zero of a holomorphic
mapping, integral representations for strictly pseudoconvex and n-circular
domains, the Schwarz kernel; the residue theory of Leray, Alexander–
Pontryagin and de Rham duality, residues of rational functions; and
applications to implicit functions, systems of nonlinear equations,
multiplicities of zeros, combinatorial sums, the Hartogs–Bochner theorem,
the ∂̄-problem and Oka's theorems, forms orthogonal to holomorphic
functions, and bases of spaces of holomorphic functions.

The Typst edition keeps the pagination of the front matter, and its
chapters open on the pages of the 1983 edition; the page breaks within them
differ. It has a linked contents, bibliography (with
DOIs), subject index and index of notation. Errors of the 1983 edition are
corrected, including the correction notice of the publisher (the first
author is L. A., not I. A., Aĭzenberg); editorial notes explain the few
corrections a reader needs explained, and their literature is listed as
Editorial References after the bibliography. Each release includes the
reader's PDF and a separate list of corrections. `checks/` holds exact
computations in SageMath and formal steps in Lean that support the
corrections (see [checks/README.md](checks/README.md)). See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for source and build conventions.
